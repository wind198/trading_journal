# Interactive trade hypothesis — phase 3b

After an on-demand scan, choose long/short and arm Entry, SL or TP with its crosshair button. Click/tap the main price pane in M3 or M15. The chart converts the y-coordinate to price, snaps to the snapshot point size and records the source timeframe for entry. Each pick is one-shot; Escape or pressing the active picker cancels. Ordinary chart interaction is unchanged while disarmed. Indicator pane clicks are ignored. Existing levels are synchronized across both charts using price lines. Move a level by rearming and selecting a new location; direct drag handles are deferred. Numeric inputs provide a keyboard-accessible alternative. No auto-generated SL/TP. Changing direction preserves prices and flags invalid geometry.

Hypothesis state is independent of the trading dashboard and order runtime. Every committed change increments revision; new snapshots retain chosen prices and increment revision. Clear creates a new hypothesis ID. Gross point distances and reward/risk update immediately; missing or invalid inputs never imply a safe trade. No monetary risk, fees, AI, or actual structural evaluation yet. No persistence or new dependencies.
