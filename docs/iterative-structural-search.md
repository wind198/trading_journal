# Iterative structural search

Submit starts a dedicated module Web Worker with the frozen workspace snapshot and confirmed hypothesis. No fetches, order execution, or automatic trade evaluation. Five stages: baseline, 2x, 4x, alternative anchors, buffer sensitivity. Candidate pairs remain within one timeframe and scale. Range boundary pairs never mix inner/outer or zone/boundary anchors. Larger-scale range pairs require usable choppy profile and entry inside range. Active zone pairs and intact confirmed extrema (loss side) extend discovery; pivot stops opt-in only. Buffers 0.15/0.25/0.5 ATR with original target buffer. Existing evaluator and hard policy unchanged; every retained candidate receives complete review, including failures. Target 5 combined, geometry diversity >=20% on either entry-relative risk or reward distance (larger distance / smaller distance >=1.2, symmetric, compared against every retained passed or failed candidate), max 100 full evaluations, cooperative 2s budget checked between stages and candidates (not a hard wall-clock cap; entry review/stage enumeration may overrun). Max 8 anchors per side per assessment. Stop reason, stage counts and near-duplicate skip count reported; attempted raw combinations can include duplicates across stages. Progress in review sheet; cancellation terminates worker and discards unfinished results; new snapshot/entry/direction cancels old worker; stale worker messages ignored. No fallback blocking main thread. Initial five distinct candidates are not optimized or guaranteed to include a passing setup; wider range feasibility still uses existing baseline evaluator gates, so broader geometry can be rejected by baseline structural infeasibility. Thresholds experimental. Two fixtures appended; tests, worker bundling, build and browser not executed. Installed Next docs unavailable. No dependencies/migrations.

## Relative diversity (policy 0.3.0)
A 50-point stop and 100-point target are equivalent to 59/119 but distinct from 60/100 or 50/120. Absolute FX price, source scale and outcome do not bypass the geometry test. Search continues through remaining stages if similar candidates are skipped. No threshold relaxation to reach five. Tests updated but not run.

## Expanded coverage (0.4.0)
Six stages including deep history. Scan independently validated/provisional ranges of 50–600 bars and active zones/intact pivot pools of 100–600 bars, requiring contiguous history. Pair within each pool; no mixed range boundaries or pivot/zone pairs for range. 24 anchors per side sampled across price distance. Stop buffers 0.15/0.25/0.5/0.75/1 ATR and target buffers 1/3/5/10 points inside existing anchor. Budget 4 seconds cooperative, 200 full evaluations; stage enumeration itself can overrun. Seek five total, preferably at least two passing if available: passing candidate can replace similar failed candidates, maintaining pairwise 20% separation. Stage counts are net retained, not total discoveries. RR hard policy 1.5–3 inclusive; above 3 blocked and visually explained, not silently truncated. Historical cutoff unchanged. Existing baseline strategy gates still apply. Tests/build/browser not run.

## Multi-seed search and main-chart overlays (0.5.0)
Discovery reserves half of the cooperative four-second budget, deduplicates exact geometry and merges origin identifiers. Eight overlapping seed queues (local rotation, local correction, primary, extended, macro, tight/deep invalidation, equilibrium) prioritize farthest-first representatives per hard-policy outcome. Round-robin evaluates at least two rounds when available before quota termination; final selection uses log-distance and strict pairwise 20% diversity. Prepared evaluator caches phase, context, baseline structure, choppy ranges and force observations, not geometry-dependent warnings. Search-anchor pivots cached per timeframe; range-validation pivots still separately computed. Stage/assessment enumeration and seed shortlist construction remain synchronous and may overrun cooperative budget. No indexed binary search of TP intervals yet; bounded geometry screening remains Cartesian within coherent pools. Seed membership is based on structural sources and distance, not independent randomized optimization. Main charts plot retained passed and failed setups in separate columns, using stable M3 span/ATR threshold, otherwise M15; hover/tap rationale and detailed review link, independent outcome toggles. Chart price coordinate alignment and autoscale are browser-unverified. Tests/build/worker bundling not run. No dependency or migration changes.
