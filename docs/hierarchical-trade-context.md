# Hierarchical trade context (experimental)

M15 is the structural context and M3 is the local execution context, regardless of the chart used to pick entry. Both observations remain visible. Phase detection uses closed-bar 10-bar displacement divided by current ATR, signed Kijun extension, ADX change and existing efficiency. Thresholds live in phase-policy.json, not in the hard-risk policy. Stale, discontinuous or missing recent data becomes unknown. Expansion, correction candidate, consolidation, exhaustion candidate and transition do not certify trend roots or reversals. No phase persistence or hysteresis yet.

A broader continuation with opposing M3 trend is a potential correction entry, not two unrelated trade hypotheses. For that local assessment use correction-specific adverse pressure, local pivot candidates and continuation stop/target distance thresholds; do not require countertrend exhaustion extension. M15 remains primary. Local continuation in the opposite M15 direction is a countertrend correction trade. Range with local directional move is ambiguous rotation/breakout; transition and unknown generate uncertainty warnings. Existing independent references are retained and never silently chosen as orders. Hard limits unchanged. No AI, migrations, new dependencies or automatic execution.

Phase observations are calculated against the frozen snapshot on demand; UI review follows hypothesis revisions. Evidence references point to existing indicator calculations and explicit phase lookback bounds. Full phase-specific evidence objects, active structural zones, correction root/recovery, breakout retest detection, chart reference overlays and calibration remain future work.
