# Three displayed regimes and automatic choppy bounds

Previous classifier blocked any cadence gap across 400 candles, stale snapshots and short/flat history. Now every nonempty completed candle series has uptrend, downtrend or provisional choppy. No completed data is a distinct empty state. Recent 51-bar gaps and stale data downgrade to provisional choppy and prevent trade options, while old gaps remain evidence limitations. Short history and unavailable ATR do not invent validated bounds. Display extrema use up to 50 prior bars, or the single completed candle if necessary. A flat range is one line. Choppy outer and available nested inner lines are always visible, independent of trade submission and Refs toggle; tilde means provisional. Data quality remains visible in pane header and review. No changes to hard constraints or submit-only evaluation. Scan still consumes completed candles from the existing Supabase candle provider, not raw ticks; if ticks exist but no completed candle is available, pane shows No data. Raw tick ingestion fallback is not included. No dependency/migration. Fixture changes committed but tests/build/browser not executed.
