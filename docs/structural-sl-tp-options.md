# Entry-only structural option generation

Input: snapshot-bound hypothesis entry and direction. Ignore manual SL/TP while generating alternatives; retain global entry/data warnings. Unknown, transition, unresolved range breakout or unassessed evidence withhold options. Pair only same-timeframe, primary-strategy-compatible references (local correction entry is compatible with continuation). Stops require active zone outer edge, provisional recovered correction root or validated range boundary; historical pivots alone do not establish stop invalidation. Targets use structural obstacles or countertrend equilibrium; range pairs stay within validated boundaries. Buffer SL beyond reference by max(one point, 0.15 ATR); TP one point before reference. Direction-aware snapping. Never adjust a pair to force policy compliance. Re-run full deterministic review and exclude blocked, infeasible or incomplete options. Deduplicate and return up to three in stable ascending risk distance then target distance order; this is enumeration, not quality ranking. No automatic selection or order placement. A chart preview copies both prices atomically only if snapshot/hypothesis/revision still match.

Experimental buffers and references require calibration. No-feasible means no supported pair from this implementation, not proof no trade exists. Candidate coverage limited by existing strategy references; no certified reversal extreme or breakout invalidation detector. Monetary risk/costs unassessed.
