# Daily Prep & Schedule

Workday timetable for EURUSD scalping (FTMO 10k 1-Step). All times **UTC+7** (`Asia/Ho_Chi_Minh`).

Related: [context](../context.md) · [decision flow](make_trade/trading_decision_flow.md) · [up](make_trade/trade_up_trend.md) · [down](make_trade/trade_down_trend.md) · [chop](make_trade/trade_choppy_range.md)

## Mode legend

| Mode | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| PREP | Mark levels, bias, plan — no new entries |
| TRADE | Allowed to take setups if decision flow passes |
| NO TRADE | Hard off — commute, work focus, or app non-trade |
| MANAGE | Manage / flatten only — no new entries |
| JOURNAL | Log day, set next watch levels |

## Hard rules

- Trade **slow with focus**. If distracted, late, or rushing → WAIT.
- **No commute / breakfast / walk trading** (07:45–09:30). Traffic waits → notes only, no execute.
- No new entries in app non-trade: **09:30–11:45**, **14:30–19:35**.
- No entry without [decision flow](make_trade/trading_decision_flow.md) pass.
- TP from **50 points**; skip if RR cannot support that under playbook SL.
- Morning TRADE hard cutoff **07:45** — no new entries after that; flatten or hard-manage by then. Leave-home time is negotiable around the cutoff.

## Session focus notes

### Morning (06:00–07:45) — primary quality window

Best personal conditions: home, awake, no commute pressure. Prefer fewer trades, higher selectivity. Asia can be quieter early — wait for clear levels; do not invent momentum. Big morning moves often show only after an initial correction; keep the window open until **07:45** rather than rushing the leave.

### Noon (12:00–13:20)

Short clock. Prep must be done by 12:00. If Plan A not ready → no trade. Hard cutoff 13:20.

### Evening (19:35–21:30)

Higher activity (London/NY). Still trade slow: confirmation over FOMO. Respect countertrend wait at major opens per decision flow.

## Quick checklist (every TRADE block)

1. Context still valid? (EURUSD, risk room, TP ≥ 50pt possible)
2. Decision flow: regime → setup → not WAIT
3. Sitting focused (not rushing / multitasking)?
4. Entry / SL / TP / buffer per playbook
5. Time left in window for manage (not just entry)?
