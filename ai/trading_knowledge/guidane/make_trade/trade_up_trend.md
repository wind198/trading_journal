# Trading the Uptrend

## When
- Primary TF:
- Regime: uptrend
- HTF context: A bigger up trend (this is a sub wave), A choppy market (this is an in-range move) or A down trend (this is a correction up trend)

## Thesis
There is generally no trade until price enters a correction. That correction can last a long time, so the trade itself follows the choppy-market playbook.
But trade before the up trend is well established is also considered risky, as down correct is often strong, moving fast, so the SL can easily get eaten up

## Trend following playbook
- Entry: In the ideal scenario, all strong key level in the correction should be well tested with positive test result, the key reversal key level of the correction should be tested and close above or test result should be bullish. The reaction of bulls should be strong and grow, not truncated immediately. We enter with a market order or close limit order to ensure we enter in the right moment, not get caught in the choppy market.
- SL: initially target the root of the uptrend, more room than in a downtrend because corrections are deeper, avoid trailing SL as the correction in uptrend are always deepter than down trend correction, so the SL will easily get eaten up
- TP: target the previous high of the uptrend, extend to high time frame resistance level
- Order: buy; expect standard TP or slightly extended TP, not targeting highly extended TP like in downtrend
- Buffer: easier to size correctly than in a downtrend because the move is slower

### Wait / no-trade
- No trade opportunity until a correction begins
- No trade untile all strong key level and key reversal level of the correction are well tested with positive test result

### Recognition
- The existing trend has strong marubozu acting as roots
- The correctio unable to penetrate the root of the uptrend, key levels of the correction are well tested with positive test result

### Variants
#### Long correction
A correction lasts longer when it is strong enough that bullish trend participants reconsider and become more conservative. Bulls then need time to rebuild momentum. Price may repeatedly test key levels inside the corrective range before the uptrend resumes.

A strong correction with growing Marubozu candles during 2 of 3 phases of the corrective wave often leads to an extended choppy market.

#### Short correction
A correction tends to be shorter when selling pressure is weak relative to the prevailing bullish momentum:

- Weak selling: the sell side fails to produce strong Marubozu candles during 2 of 3 phases of the corrective wave.
- Overwhelming buying: strong bullish Engulfing patterns appear near the bottom of the correction; buyers are absorbing selling pressure and may resume the uptrend.

### Traps
- Root of uptrend look strong, but the reaction at it become week and the correction just fall through, completely reverse the trend

## Counter trend playbook
- Entry: when the uptrend is extended. How to detect extension? It' an area of consecutive maruobozu that not come from a strong consolidation, known as 3 sword pattern but do not originate from a long consolidation that make them look strong but actually vulnerable. The 3 sword pattern then followed by doji, truncated maruboze, this appear at a higher level timeframe resistance is the common sight of an extended uptrend. We enter with a limit order with a proper buffer to ensure we have a safe position.
- SL: put SL above the high timeframe resistance level
- TP: standard TP at the first strong resistance level, no extension on counter trend order

### Wait / no-trade
- Wait for the truncated marubozu, doji to appear, no matter how far the trend has extended, we do not trade until this pattern appears
- Avoid trading the counter trend the beginning of major markets (like London and New York market), as the momentum is often strong when these market open, the trend may continue to extend even more, so we wait for the market to stabilize before trading the counter trend

### Recognition
- Focus on detecting trend extension that coincide with the high timeframe resistance leve
