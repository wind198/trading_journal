# Trading the Downtrend

## When
- Primary TF:
- Regime: downtrend
- HTF context: A bigger down trend (this is a sub wave), A choppy market (this is an in-range move) or An up trend (this is a correction down trend)

## Thesis
A strong downtrend moves faster than an uptrend. Corrections are often short and shallower, so the SL is often not safe. So the workable approach is only enter when the downtrend is forming, or during a long consolidation phase, not chasing the downtrend or try to catch a short correction

## Trend creating playbook
- Entry: when the downtrend is forming (often at tested HTF resistance). What we expect is a distributing zone where bull size try to push though multiple time at failed, indicated by truncated (not growing) bull Marubozu, doji. We also expect this zone to be appear after and extended up trend.
- SL: Initially put above the tested HTF resistance, then trail along the position
- TP: Initially target the closest resistance level lower to the distrubuted zone, then extend to high timeframe resistance level
- Order: sell; hold as long as possible
- Buffer: harder to size correctly than in an uptrend because the move is faster, so we use a bigger buffer, expecting a good position that can survive a choppy distribution phase.

### Wait / no-trade
- For a down trend to be created, a distributed zone is a must, any sudden false of the previous up trend should be considered as a correction, not a new down trend until market enter a choppy phase for distribution.
- We should not expect a down trend to be created at the beginning of a major market, as the momentum is often high so it will take time for the distribution zone to form.

### Recognition
- Levels: previous HTF resistance, tested multiple times by the bull side. More tests → stronger downtrend after, appear after an extended up trend
- Candles: truncated (not growing) bull Marubozu at that resistance, doji
- Other:

### Traps
- Some consolidation zone might be confused as a distribution zone, in the end , to distinguish, be must compare the reaction between 2 boundary of the choppy market, else wait for breakout then retest.

## Trend following playbook
- Entry: Down trend often move fast, so if the correction is short and fast, just let it go, dont chase it. What is trend following trying to target is the deep, long correction, often appear in some fighting market, not just a fall-through down trend. We wait for the correction of the down trend to coming to and end, typically an ABC wave that end with some flusing, indicating by truncated (not growing) bear Marubozu, doji.
- SL: Initially target the root of the downtrend, then trail along the position
- TP: Initially target the previous low of the downtrend, then extend to high timeframe support level
- Buffer: In trend following, the buffer is smaller, as we enter with a market order or close limit order to ensure we enter in the right moment, not get caught in the choppy market.


### Wait / no-trade
- We need to wait until all strong key level and key reversal level of the correction are well tested with positive test result, the key reversal level should be tested and close below or test result should be bearish. The reaction of bears should be strong and grow, not truncated immediately.
Typically show up in a double rejection pattern

### Traps
- Avoid enter the trade when all the needed test result are available and review with positive test result, get caught in the choppy market.
